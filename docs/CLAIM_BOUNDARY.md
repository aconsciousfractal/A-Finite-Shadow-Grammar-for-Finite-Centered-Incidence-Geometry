## Promoted interface claims

1. A concrete finite-shadow row is the five-slot datum `S=(N,X,I,rho,Phi)`, with `X` a finite subset of `S_N` and `N` the ambient permutation degree.
2. IDs `X01` through `X26` label source/family slots. They do not name individual finite subsets, and a family parameter such as `n` is distinct from the concrete ambient degree `N`.
3. Admitted concrete rows are governed by normative source-lock metadata for provenance, action conventions, channels, versions, verification, and import boundaries. The package supplies a schema-to-surface routing crosswalk, not a proof that every field value is consolidated in one place, and does not assert one consolidated `L(S)` object for every row. Source-lock metadata is not a sixth slot and does not assert a universal category of FCIG objects.
4. The standard-block balance dictionary is used as an elementary representation-theoretic interface: position-value balance corresponds to vanishing of the standard Specht block in the stated permutation channel.
5. `Phi` is a descriptive, source-bounded fingerprint package. It is not a complete invariant, classifier, tiling oracle, or replacement for a decision certificate.
6. The modular row records characteristic, augmentation channel, reduced quotient when applicable, and lattice-qualified `SNF^L`; it is a bounded exhibit, not an all-layer modular theorem.
7. The Type-A, signed Type-B/C, dihedral, G2, F4, H3, H4, D5, E6, E7, and E8 rows are bounded source-aware exhibits. They do not promote family-wide Coxeter, Weyl, or classification theorems.
8. The H4 row uses the same 60 projective points, equivalently 60 antipodal pairs of H4 roots. Its flat-size/Gram-square comparison is the controlling public wording.
9. The M19/X05 row is a bounded frozen-evidence and `lat565` witness exhibit. The default route audits the frozen 1000+1000 census; optional full regeneration is not a mechanism proof.
10. `G-004/CPL-001` records the retained S36/S37 source-coupling/descent material only as a classical guardrail supported by human proof and sources. It creates no X-ID or numerical route and claims no new coupling, descent, transfer, or shared-prime theorem.
