5. `Phi` is a descriptive, source-bounded fingerprint package. It is not a complete invariant, classifier, tiling oracle, or replacement for a decision certificate.
6. The modular row records characteristic, augmentation channel, reduced quotient when applicable, and lattice-qualified `SNF^L`; it is a bounded exhibit, not an all-layer modular theorem.
7. The Type-A, X15-001 signed Type-B/C, dihedral, G2, F4, H3, H4, D5, E6, E7, and E8 numerical rows are bounded source-aware exhibits. X15-002 is only the separately proved all-`n` mirror-union balance identity; it is not a Type-B/C classification or broader Coxeter/Weyl theorem.

10. `G-004/CPL-001` records the retained S36/S37 source-coupling/descent material only as a classical guardrail supported by human proof and sources. It creates no X-ID or numerical route and claims no new coupling, descent, transfer, or shared-prime theorem.
