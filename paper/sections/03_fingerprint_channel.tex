\section{The Fingerprint Channel \texorpdfstring{\(\Phi\)}{Phi}}

For \(X\subseteq S_N\), the fingerprint channel records block data attached to the Specht/Fourier transforms
\[
  M_\lambda(X)=\sum_{x\in X}D^\lambda(x),
\]
where \(D^\lambda\) is a fixed integral Specht model for the partition \(\lambda\vdash N\).  The background representation theory is standard\cite{Diaconis1988,JamesKerber1981}.

\begin{definition}[the \(\Phi\)-package]
The package \(\PhiFCIG(X)\) may include rational ranks \(\rank_\mathbb{Q} M_\lambda(X)\), the lattice-qualified invariant \(\SNFL_\lambda(X)\), selected finite-field ranks, block-energy data, transport metadata, and rank mass.  Every Smith normal form entry is lattice-qualified; no bare Smith form is used as an invariant independent of the chosen integral model.  The use of Smith normal form is standard combinatorial linear algebra\cite{StanleySmithNormalForm2016}; the superscript in \(\SNFL\) records that the lattice has been fixed.
\end{definition}

The following elementary dictionary is the main reason the grammar is useful rather than decorative.  It connects the centered-operator language to a specific Specht block.

\begin{proposition}[standard-block balance dictionary]\label{prop:standard-block-balance}
Let \(X\subseteq S_N\), and let \(P_x\) be the permutation matrix of \(x\) on \(\mathbb{C}^N\), with rows indexed by values and columns by positions.  Put
\[
  N_X=\sum_{x\in X}P_x,\qquad
  E_X=N_X-\frac{|X|}{N}J_N,
\]
where \(J_N\) is the all-ones matrix.  Then the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)},leftmargin=*]
  \item \(X\) is position-value balanced, i.e. \(N_X=(|X|/N)J_N\);
  \item \(E_X\) vanishes on the standard subspace \(\Vstd=\{v:\sum_i v_i=0\}\);
  \item the \((N-1,1)\) Specht block \(M_{(N-1,1)}(X)\) vanishes, after choosing the corresponding standard model.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The permutation representation decomposes as \(\mathbb{C}^N=\mathbb{C}\mathbf{1}\oplus\Vstd\).  The constant line records the total row and column sums, while the quotient block is the Specht module of shape \((N-1,1)\).  Subtracting \((|X|/N)J_N\) removes the constant-line part.  Thus the centered matrix vanishes exactly when its standard block vanishes.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[classical left-ideal reading]\label{prop:left-ideal-rankmass}
Let \(d_\lambda=\dim S^\lambda\).  For \(X\subseteq S_N\), the rank mass used in \(\PhiFCIG\) satisfies
\[
  \rankmass(X)=\sum_{\lambda\vdash N} d_\lambda\,\rank_\mathbb{Q}M_\lambda(X)
  =\dim_\mathbb{C}\bigl(\mathbb{C}[S_N] \oneX\bigr),
\]
where \(\oneX=\sum_{x\in X}x\in\mathbb{C}[S_N]\).  This is the standard Wedderburn/Fourier interpretation of the left ideal generated by the indicator element\cite{Serre1977,Diaconis1988}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}
\Cref{prop:left-ideal-rankmass} is background, not a novelty claim.  Its role is to make the fingerprint channel auditable: when rank mass compresses or fails to compress, the statement is a statement about a classical group-algebra left ideal.
\end{remark}
