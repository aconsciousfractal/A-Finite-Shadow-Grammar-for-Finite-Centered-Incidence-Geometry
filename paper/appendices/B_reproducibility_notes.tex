\section{Reproducibility Notes}
\label{app:reproducibility}

This repository contains the LaTeX source, compiled title-named PDF, public boundary notes, source manifest, public replay artifacts, and SHA-256 manifest for the paper snapshot.  The paper is an interface paper: its finite numerical rows are replayable in the cited public companion repositories or in bounded companion artifacts summarized by \cref{app:source-manifest}.  No theorem in this paper depends on treating those companion artifacts as public mathematical authority.

The paper can be rebuilt from \path{paper/main.tex} with the title-named job target
\path{A_Finite-Shadow_Grammar_for_Finite_Centered_Incidence_Geometry}.  Use \texttt{pdflatex} with that \texttt{-jobname} value, then \texttt{bibtex} on the same job name, followed by three additional \texttt{pdflatex} passes with the same \texttt{-jobname} option.  The expected output is
\[
\texttt{paper/A\_Finite-Shadow\_Grammar\_for\_Finite\_Centered\_Incidence\_Geometry.pdf}.
\]
