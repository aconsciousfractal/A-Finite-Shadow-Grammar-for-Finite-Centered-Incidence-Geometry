\section{Reproducibility Notes}
\label{app:reproducibility}

This repository contains the LaTeX source, compiled title-named PDF, public boundary notes, source manifest, and SHA-256 manifest for the paper snapshot.  The paper is an interface paper: its finite numerical rows are replayable in the cited public companion repositories or in bounded companion artifacts summarized by \cref{app:source-manifest}.  No theorem in this paper depends on treating those companion artifacts as public mathematical authority.

The paper can be rebuilt from \path{paper/main.tex} with the standard sequence
\[
\texttt{pdflatex, bibtex, pdflatex, pdflatex}.
\]
The expected output is
\[
\texttt{paper/A\_Finite-Shadow\_Grammar\_for\_Finite\_Centered\_Incidence\_Geometry.pdf}.
\]
