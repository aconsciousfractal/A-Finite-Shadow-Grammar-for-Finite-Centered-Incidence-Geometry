\section{Source and Instance Manifest}
\label{app:source-manifest}

This appendix records the finite-shadow instance matrix used by the paper.  Public scholarly rows are cited in the bibliography.  Companion artifact rows are included only as bounded source rows for the interface; they are not cited as independent mathematical authority unless separately released.

The third column below gives a descriptive registry role and, where relevant, pointer posture.  It is not the canonical admission type.  Canonical admission is typed separately as \texttt{admitted} or \texttt{not\_admitted}, with an independent \texttt{pointer\_status}, in \path{artifacts/source_instance_manifest.json}.
