This paper records a bounded finite-shadow grammar for finite centered incidence geometry.  A row is admitted as a tuple \(S=(n,X,I,\rho,\PhiFCIG)\), where \(X\) is a finite subset of a symmetric group, or a source-locked finite shadow inside one; \(I\) is the incidence rule; \(\rho\) is the representation or centered-operator channel; and \(\PhiFCIG\) is a replayable fingerprint package.  The payoff is not a universal theory: it is a controlled interface that makes the same standard-block and fingerprint tests visible across the published Type-A row, the Fourier/Specht artifact, a signed Type-B/C shadow, and a bounded centered-operator exhibit.  The standard-block dictionary is elementary, but it becomes useful here because it is applied on both sides: to the Type-A one-incidence layer and to a signed mirror-union row.  The paper includes worked finite evidence showing that \(\PhiFCIG\) is not determined by coarse cycle-type histograms, while also recording why it is not a complete invariant, not a tiling oracle, and not a substitute for the separate theorem routes.
